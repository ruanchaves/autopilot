Where each field comes from

  • Accept / Reject decisions are saved with this page and kept across daily updates. Each one stores a copy of the role, so accepted and rejected roles stay listed after later sweeps stop finding them. Click the highlighted button again to undo.
  • Autopilot · Applications lists every form the autopilot filled and left open in Chrome. It never submits. Review the typed answers (estimates are flagged), submit the form yourself, then click I submitted it; the next run registers it in applika. Discard closes a form you won't send.
  • Autopilot · Outreach is the recruiter pipeline, separate from applications: recruiters found on LinkedIn, scored on whether they hire in or worldwide, are active, and post roles and pay like your target, with the exact message sent to each, the search that found them, and what they did next (accepted, replied, interested, declined). "Which searches work" above the list shows that funnel per search and per closing style.
  • eligibility is read from the job description text. "Open" means it names , or worldwide eligibility; "Restricted" means it states a visa, citizenship or residency requirement (e.g. "must live in the US"); "Nothing stated" means neither. Roles without JD text are "Not classified".
  • Resume fit rates the JD against . Only roles classified "Open" or "Nothing stated" are rated.
  • hiring check is one Google search per company, , first page only, run for the green and yellow roles with nothing stated (results are cached per company, so other roles from a checked company show them too). A match shows the company employs people there; it doesn't prove the role is open to . "No match" means only that the first page had none.
  • Sweep batch is the day a sweep ran. A role found again by a later sweep belongs to every batch that found it, so picking one batch shows everything that sweep turned up. The preset views keep the batch you picked; "Clear filters" resets it.
  • Region comes from the JD classification when there is one, otherwise from the posted location tag. Seniority is read from the title. Posted age is measured from when this page was built ().
  • Best match first sorts by eligibility (open, nothing stated, not classified, restricted), then resume fit, then the check, then newest.
